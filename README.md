# Equity-Research-Analyser 📊

Equity-Research-Analyser is a **LangChain-based application** that enables equity researchers and investors to analyze financial articles by simply providing their URLs. Users can ask natural language questions and receive **actionable insights related to the stock market and financial domain**, helping improve research efficiency and decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🔗 **URL-based Article Analysis**  
  Input one or multiple financial news or research article URLs.

- 🧠 **LLM-Powered Insights**  
  Uses **LangChain** to process content and generate domain-specific answers.

- ❓ **Question Answering System**  
  Ask questions related to stocks, companies, financial performance, risks, outlook, and more.

- ⚡ **Faster Equity Research**  
  Reduces manual reading time and improves productivity for analysts and investors.

---

## Libraries used

1. Langchain
2. Langchain text_splitter
3. Langchain document_loaders
4. Langchain embeddings
5. Langchain vectorstores
